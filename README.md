### About me

I build cloud-native backend systems and, increasingly, the AI agents that operate
them. At Royal Cyber I work on **RC AI-Ops**, migrating an enterprise AIOps
platform onto Amazon Bedrock — runtime agent/tool creation, MCP servers, and
integrations with IBM MQ and Datadog. Before that I spent five months as a
Data Science Intern building web-crawling pipelines and agentic automation
with n8n and Crawl4AI.

I'm most interested in the space where **DevOps meets AI**: agents that can
safely reason about infrastructure and act on it, instead of engineers
context-switching between fifteen dashboards during an incident.

- 🔭 Currently building **CloudOps AI Agent** — a Slack bot that turns
  natural-language ops requests into whitelisted, audited AWS/Kubernetes
  actions.
- 🌱 Deepening my Kubernetes, Terraform, and Amazon Bedrock/agentic-AI skills.
- 🏆 National Finalist, Smart India Hackathon 2023 — top 5 of 1,282 teams,
  presented to the Prime Minister of India.
- 💬 Ask me about agentic workflows (LangChain/LangGraph/CrewAI), serverless
  architectures on AWS, or migrating automation platforms between providers.
